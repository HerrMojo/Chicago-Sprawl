# PRESS — Newspaper Generator

AI-powered dual newspaper generator for CBR+PNK sessions.

## Two Publications

**THE CORP PAPER** — sleek corporate broadsheet. Sanitizes events, spins deaths, praises order. Includes animated stock ticker and financial report reflecting session events.

**THE UNDERGROUND ZINE** — rough, hand-assembled street zine. Tells the truth. Raw, angry, dangerous to read.

## Setup

1. Upload `index.html` to your GitHub Pages repo (can be in a `/press/` subfolder)
1. Access at `https://yourusername.github.io/repo-name/press/`

## How to Use

1. Select **Corp Paper** or **Underground Zine** using the toggle
1. Enter your **City Name** and **Publication Name**
1. Set the **Issue Number** and **in-world date**
1. For Corp Paper: fill in stock ticker data (corp names, share prices, % change)
1. Paste your **session notes** — be as detailed as you want. Include:
- What the run objective was
- Who the runners were (handles)
- What corps/NPCs were involved
- Events, twists, deaths
- The outcome
1. Hit **Generate Issue**
1. **Print / Save PDF** to save the issue, or **Copy HTML** to host it as a page

## Publishing Issues

Each generated issue is a standalone HTML file. To publish:

1. Copy the HTML
1. Save as `issue-01-corp.html` or `issue-01-zine.html`
1. Upload to your GitHub Pages repo
1. Link from the Sessions tab in your city wiki

## Stock Ticker Tips

- Add stocks for every major corp in your city
- Adjust prices up/down based on how they fared in the session
- If runners hit a corp facility → their stock drops
- If a corp covered something up successfully → stock is stable or up
- This becomes a living economic record of your city

## Notes

The AI interprets the same events through two completely different lenses:

- The corp paper will protect powerful interests
- The zine will name names and call out lies

Contradictions between the two papers are features, not bugs.
